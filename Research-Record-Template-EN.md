## 12. Pre-Submission Checklist

Confirm that:

- [ ] The research question or objective is clearly stated

## Additional Notes

- It does not need to be long, but others need to be able to understand it
- If one session of work covers a lot, you can split it into multiple records
- If your research type has special needs, you can add fields, but do not delete the core fields

> The purpose of this template is to let different types of research accumulate and continue under the same management framework.
