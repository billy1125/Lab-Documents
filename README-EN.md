## About

This repository contains the basic policies and documents needed to run the lab. The goal is for all members to do research and daily work under the same clear set of expectations.

Please understand the following:

- Policies exist to reduce communication costs and avoid unnecessary misunderstandings  
- Policies cannot cover every situation, but they provide basic guidelines for conduct  
- If a policy does not fit the actual situation, raise it for discussion instead of interpreting or ignoring it on your own  

## Intended Readers

This document applies to:

- Students who have not joined yet but are considering it
- Students who have just joined the lab or a project team
- Current members of the lab or a project team

## Important Reminders

- Policies will change over time or as the lab's situation changes  
- Not every change will be announced individually  
- Please check back regularly for the latest version  

## Shared Understanding

In this lab, what we want to build is not a system of close monitoring, but:

> **An environment where people manage themselves and take responsibility under clear policies**

This means:

- You need to actively understand the policies, not wait to be reminded  
- If you have a question, ask it instead of making assumptions  
- Policies are the minimum standard, not the goal  

## Finally

If you have questions about any policy or think something is unreasonable:

- Please bring it up for discussion directly  
- Most problems can be solved through communication  

But this assumes:

> **You have read the relevant documents and tried to understand them.**
