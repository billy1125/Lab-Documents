# Lab Equipment Management and Borrowing Policy

> Disclaimer: This policy will be adjusted according to how the lab operates.

## 1. Let's Be Clear First

This lab manages equipment under an **honor system**.

This rests on one basic premise:

> **Manage any equipment you borrow as if it were your own property.**

No one will check on you all the time, and we will not restrict you with heavy-handed management. In return, we expect you to take responsibility for your own actions.

Equipment is not a consumable, and it is not a resource to "use until it breaks and deal with it later." It is an asset that must be used properly and maintained over the long term.
## 2. Basic Responsibilities When Borrowing

### 1. Borrowing = Responsibility

To borrow any equipment, whether or not it has a university property sticker, you must fill out a "**borrowing form**." The borrowing form is in our cloud drive. Download it and fill it out yourself.

When you complete the borrowing registration and sign it:

- You need to know exactly which equipment you borrowed
- This includes the main unit and all accessories
- You are responsible for the equipment during the borrowing period
- At the start of every semester, you must re-confirm the items, fill out a new borrowing form, and sign it

This covers:

- How it is used
- How it is stored and kept
- Whether anything is lost or damaged

> If you do not know what you borrowed, the borrowing process itself has a problem and must be clarified immediately.

### 2. Basic Principles of Use

Follow these principles:

- Use it only for research or lab-related purposes
- Use the equipment in a normal, reasonable way
- Do not perform operations whose risks you have not confirmed
- Do not disassemble, modify, or alter the equipment on your own

Also:

- Do not lend it to others without permission
- If someone else needs to use it, there must be a clear record

### 3. Completeness of Accessories (Pay Special Attention)

Every time you borrow and return equipment, you need to check:

- The main unit
- All accessories (cables, power supplies, adapters, etc.)

The principle is simple:

> **Return everything you borrowed.**

The following should not happen:

- Forgetting which accessories came with it
- Losing some parts without dealing with it
- Thinking "small items don't matter"

Whether most equipment is usable depends on whether its accessories are complete.

## 3. Regular Checks and Self-Management

### 1. Equipment Check at the Start of Each Semester

Each student must:

- Confirm the condition of the equipment with the professor at the start of each semester
- Check the equipment and accessories currently on loan
- Confirm the equipment works properly
- Fill out a new borrowing form and sign it

The purpose of this process is to avoid:

- Loss or damage that goes unnoticed for a long time
- Unclear responsibility
- Not knowing the state of the equipment

### 2. Routine Self-Checks

Besides the semester check, also do the following yourself:

- Check the condition of the equipment regularly
- Confirm all accessories are present
- If you have not used it for a long time, consider on your own whether you should return it

## 4. Handling Damage and Problems

### 1. What to Do When Something Goes Wrong

When equipment shows any of the following:

- Malfunction
- Abnormal behavior
- Damage
- Missing accessories

Please:

> **Tell the professor right away.**

The earlier it is handled, the easier it usually is to fix.

### 2. What Not to Do

Avoid the following:

- Disassembling or repairing the equipment yourself
- Having someone else handle it without telling the professor
- Delaying the report or choosing not to say anything
- Assuming "it should still work"

These behaviors tend to make the problem worse and make it hard to determine responsibility afterward.

### 3. Responsibility

If any of the following happens:

- Damage that was not reported
- Equipment problems caused by improper use
- Lost equipment or accessories

You may need to:

> **Take responsibility for restoring it to its original condition (repair or compensation).**

This is a basic responsibility, not an extra punishment.

## 5. Returning Equipment

When returning equipment, complete the following:

- Fill in the return date
- Confirm who is returning it
- Record the condition of the equipment (normal / abnormal)
- Have the receiver confirm it

### Return Standards

The equipment must:

- Work properly (or have any problems explained in advance)
- Have no obvious visible damage
- Have all accessories

### Cases That Do Not Meet the Standards

The following will be treated as an incomplete return:

- Missing accessories
- Damage not explained
- The condition of the equipment cannot be confirmed

## 6. Finally, Please Check the Following

Avoid the following ways of thinking:

### ❌ "It belongs to the lab, so I can use it however I want"

→ Equipment is a shared resource, but it must be used responsibly

### ❌ "I'll deal with it when it breaks"

→ Problems should be handled right away

### ❌ "Nobody's watching, so it doesn't matter"

→ The honor system is based on self-management

### ❌ "Accessories don't matter"

→ Accessories usually determine whether the equipment works properly

### ⭕ The Key Points

Remember these three things:

1. **Borrowing means responsibility**
2. **If there is a problem, report it immediately**
3. **Return everything you borrowed (including all accessories)**

## 8. Additional Notes

If any part of this policy is unclear or unreasonable in practice:

- Raise it directly for discussion
- Most problems can be improved through communication

The purpose of this policy is not to add restrictions. It is to keep equipment usable and stable over the long term, and to make everyone's responsibilities clear.
