# Suggested Master's Thesis Timeline

> Disclaimer: The following content may change over time or in response to events.

## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document breaks the development paths in Section 6 of the Lab Orientation (Please-Read-First) into concrete milestones, required deliverables, and acceptance criteria.

> **The schedule below is a process I recommend, not a mandatory requirement.**

In practice, few students follow the timeline exactly at every stage. Some take longer to settle on a research topic but still finish the thesis draft on time; others face major delays in data collection but catch up through their own effort.

- Falling behind at some stage is normal and does not mean you cannot graduate  
- When you fall behind, talk with the supervisor early and adjust the remaining plan together  
- As long as you keep making progress, you can still catch up  

> **A milestone is approved based on the quality and feasibility of the submitted work, not just because the scheduled date has arrived.**

- The schedule below uses **Path A (Standard Research Track, graduation in about 2 years)** as the baseline  
- Months are counted from enrollment  
- For other paths, see "5. Timeline Comparison for Other Paths"  

## 2. Milestones

### M1: Research Topic Confirmation

**Suggested timing: By Month 6**

Required deliverables:

- Literature review matrix (at least 20 relevant papers)  
- Research gap and research questions  
- Preliminary conceptual framework  
- Proposed research method  
- 3–5-page proposal summary  

Acceptance criteria:

- The research question is specific and academically meaningful  
- It falls within a research scope agreed upon by both the student and the supervisor  
- It is feasible within two years  

### M2: Research Design and Thesis Proposal Draft

**Suggested timing: By Month 12**

Required deliverables:

- Thesis proposal draft  
- Hypotheses and theoretical rationale (where applicable)  
- Constructs and operational definitions  
- Survey or experimental protocol  
- Working system prototype (if needed)  
- Pilot-test report and revision log  
- Ethics review submission or approval (where applicable)  

Acceptance criteria:

- The research design is executable  
- Measures and procedures are justified  
- The pilot test identifies and addresses major problems  
- If ethics review is required, approval must be obtained before participant recruitment or data collection  

### M3: Data Collection, Analysis, Conference Submission, and Thesis Proposal

**Suggested timing: By Month 18**

Required deliverables:

- Complete research dataset  
- Data screening and exclusion log  
- Reproducible analysis code or documented analysis procedures  
- Main result tables  
- Hypothesis-test results (where applicable)  
- Preliminary findings report  
- Academic conference submission, or complete competition entry  
- Thesis proposal  

Acceptance criteria:

- The data meet the agreed research-design and quality criteria  
- Primary analyses are complete, interpretable, and reproducible  
- A conference paper has been submitted or a competition entry has been completed, with acceptance as the goal  
- The manuscript has been reviewed by the supervisor before submission  

### M4: Thesis and Defense

**Suggested timing: Full draft by Month 21; defense in Month 24 (the final month)**

Required deliverables:

- Complete thesis draft  
- Discussion of theoretical or practical contributions and limitations  
- Revision log  
- Defense presentation  
- Final thesis incorporating required corrections  

Acceptance criteria:

- A reviewable full draft is delivered  
- Revisions, the defense, and all department and university graduation requirements are completed by the relevant deadlines  

## 3. Suggested Working Timeline (Path A)

| Period | Expected Work |
|--------|---------------|
| Months 1–6 | Read core literature; define research questions and initial framework; complete M1 |
| Months 7–12 | Develop detailed research design and instruments; build only the system functions the research needs; run the pilot test; submit ethics review (where applicable); complete the thesis proposal draft and M2 |
| Months 13–18 | Finalize instruments and protocol; collect and clean data; complete primary analyses; write and submit a conference paper or complete a competition entry; complete the thesis proposal and M3 |
| Months 19–21 | Interpret findings; write the full thesis; submit the full draft |
| Months 22–24 | Revise the thesis; apply for the defense and attend it in Month 24; submit the corrected final thesis |

## 4. Review and Supervision

- For each milestone, submit the written deliverables before the progress meeting and give a short oral summary at the meeting  
- Weekly meetings are recommended during the first semester; afterward, meetings may occur every two weeks, with more frequent reviews during data collection and thesis writing  
- Weekly progress reports follow Section 5 of the Lab Orientation and continue regardless of meeting frequency  
- A milestone is complete only after the supervisor reviews and accepts the submitted work  
- If revisions are required, submit an action list with revised deadlines  

> **The scope of any system development must support a defined research question.** Building a feature-rich platform is not, by itself, a sufficient research contribution.

> **Unexpected or non-significant results must be reported transparently.** Analytical decisions and data exclusions must be documented.

## 5. Timeline Comparison for Other Paths

The milestone contents are the same for every path; only the timing differs. Timing follows Section 6 of the Lab Orientation:

| Milestone | Path A | Path B | Path C | Path D |
|-----------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| M1 Research topic confirmation | Month 6 | About Month 12 | About Month 12 | Months 3–4 |
| M2 Thesis proposal draft | About Month 12 | About Month 18 | About Month 18 | Months 6–9 |
| M3 Conference submission or competition | About Month 18 | About Month 24 | About Month 24 (results with journal-submission potential) | Months 12–14 |
| M4 Graduation | About Month 24 | About Months 24–30 | About Months 30–36 | About Month 18 |

Notes:

- Path B: Spend the first 6 months reading literature and exploring directions; the research topic should preferably relate to the internship  
- Path C: Submit at least one journal paper before graduation and meet the "Additional Requirements for Path C" in Section 6  
- Path D: Not recommended as a default plan; the topic must be well-defined and manageable in scope  

## 6. Conditions and Dependencies

> **This schedule is a planning target, not a guarantee of graduation.**

Coursework, department degree requirements, ethics review (if required), thesis submission, and defense application deadlines must be checked separately against the latest university and department regulations.
